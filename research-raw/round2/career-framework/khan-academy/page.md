# Khan Academy — Engineering Career Development

Source: https://blog.khanacademy.org/engineering-career-development-at-khan-academy/
Doc: https://docs.google.com/document/d/1qr0d05X5-AsyDYqKRCfgGGcWSshTMd_vfTggfhDpbls/

## Dimensions
- **Skills** — what you do
- **Scope** — how broad your impact
- **Experience** — time/breadth in role

## Unique features
- Extra emphasis on evangelism, public artifacts, contributions to KA's educational content
- Iterated from Fog Creek's single-paragraph format -> "ton of additional clarity"
- "Student agency" framing: engineers should know current level, destination, intermediate steps without manager dictation

## Format
- Document-style with explanatory blog post
- Publicly forkable
- Both IC and manager paths covered (e.g., "engineering lead" is a callout)

## Goal
Framework is "fair, understood, transparent, competitive"
