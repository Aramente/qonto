# Square — Engineering Growth Framework (2023 update)

Source: https://developer.squareup.com/blog/squares-updated-growth-framework-for-engineers-and-engineering-managers/
PDFs: Square SWE Career Ladder + Block Engineering Career Ladder (ctfassets.net)

## Structure
- **Two tracks**: Individual Contributor (IC) and Engineering Manager (EM)
- **Levels**: L3-L9 (IC starts L3, EM starts L5)
- **Two sections per level**:
  - **Scope & Impact** — the defining criteria (must meet all)
  - **Behaviors** — exemplifying behaviors, organized into categories like *Technical Execution* and *Team Building*

## Principles
- Outcome-focused, not method-focused (flexibility across specializations: backend, frontend, etc.)
- No strict YoE minimums — scope and impact only
- Used consistently for hiring, promotion, performance, coaching
- Engineers expected to satisfy criteria *broadly, not uniformly* — uneven strength profiles fine
- 4 guiding tenets in 2023 update: level consistency (L6 old = L6 new), explicit expectations, outcome-focus, progressive clarity
