# Etsy — Engineering Career Ladder

Source: https://etsy.github.io/Etsy-Engineering-Career-Ladder/competencies.html

## Dimensions (5 core competencies)
1. **Delivery** — scoping, testing, shipping, initiative
2. **Domain Expertise** — technical knowledge, tools, business acumen
3. **Problem Solving** — architecture, debugging, critical thinking, decision-making
4. **Communication** — documentation, collaboration, empathy
5. **Leadership** — accountability, mentorship, decision-making

## Levels (5)
Beginner / Intermediate / Advanced / Expert / Leading Expert

## Format
- Matrix: 5 competencies x 5 levels
- Behavioral descriptions per cell
- **Cumulative**: "Mastery is cumulative" — engineers retain earlier-level skills

## Track structure
- IC-focused, no separate manager track in the public doc
- Leadership is integrated as one of 5 dimensions, applicable at all levels (not a separate path)
