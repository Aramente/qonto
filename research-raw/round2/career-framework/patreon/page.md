# Patreon — Engineering Leveling Guide

Source: https://www.gigabody.com/blog/how-patreon-levels-engineers/
Public URL (referenced): levels.patreon.com (currently unreachable)

## Design principles
- Clear level definitions for engineers + candidates
- Objective criteria for transparency and fairness
- **Depth within a level, not just up**: engineers can grow at senior+ without mandate to climb
- Tenure-based depth growth allowed
- One framework across domains (mobile, front-end, etc.) — fundamental skillsets are universal

## Structure
- Role expectations + specific skillsets + skill progression per IC level
- IC-focused public doc
- Acknowledges that not all engineers share career goals

## Notable choice
Patreon explicitly designs against "up-or-out" pressure — staying at a level with deeper expertise is a valid path. This is a strong signal for any modern framework.
