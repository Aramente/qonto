# Spotify — Squads, Tribes, Chapters, Guilds (Kniberg & Ivarsson 2012)

Source: https://blog.crisp.se/2012/11/14/henrikkniberg/scaling-agile-at-spotify
PDF: https://blog.crisp.se/wp-content/uploads/2012/11/SpotifyScaling.pdf

## The matrix
Two intersecting axes:

### Vertical (primary — feature delivery)
- **Squads** — small, cross-functional, mini-startups
- **Tribes** — collections of squads (40-150 ppl) on shared business area

### Horizontal (knowledge/skill)
- **Chapters** — same-discipline specialists across squads (e.g., all backend engineers in a tribe)
- **Guilds** — voluntary cross-tribe communities of practice

## Career growth implication
- **Chapter lead** is the people-development person:
  - Physically close to chapter members
  - Focuses on craftsmanship, personal development, motivation
  - NOT day-to-day delivery oversight (squad PO does that)
- This **separates skill development from delivery evaluation**

## Caveat
- Kniberg/Ivarsson explicitly said "this was a snapshot, not a blueprint" — Spotify itself has since moved on
- The matrix concept (people belong to BOTH a squad and a chapter) is the durable idea

## Insight for Qonto
The chapter/squad split maps cleanly to: someone has a *delivery context* (team/squad) and a *craft context* (job family/chapter). Career framework attaches to the craft side; OKRs/delivery attach to the squad side.
