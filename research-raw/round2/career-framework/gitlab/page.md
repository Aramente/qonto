# GitLab — Engineering Career Framework

Sources:
- https://handbook.gitlab.com/handbook/engineering/careers/matrix/
- https://handbook.gitlab.com/handbook/engineering/careers/matrix/development/dev/
- https://handbook.gitlab.com/job-families/engineering/development/backend/

## Structure (inferred from job-family pages)

### Levels (development job family)
- Engineer (Intermediate)
- Senior (grade 7)
- Staff
- Principal
- Engineering Manager (separate manager track)

### Job families
- Backend / Frontend / Fullstack / Infrastructure / Security / etc.
- Each has its own page with responsibilities + requirements
- A shared "Career Matrix" page sits above the job-family pages

## Notable choice
GitLab says: "Staff and above is more of a role than a level" — they prefer hiring at Senior and promoting Staff+ from within based on "outstanding work history within GitLab". This is a deliberate design choice — not all levels are externally hireable.

## Path structure
- Clear IC vs Manager fork at Senior -> Staff vs Senior -> EM
- Job-family pages enumerate responsibilities, requirements, "what makes you successful"
- Backed by an "Engineering Career Matrices Working Group" (so the framework is governed, not static)

## Insight for Qonto
GitLab's pattern of "job family page = role expectations" + "career matrix = level x competency expectations" is the cleanest separation. Job family is the *what* (responsibilities), matrix is the *how well* (level criteria).
