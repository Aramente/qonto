# Codecademy — Engineering Competencies (open source)

Source: https://github.com/Codecademy/engineering-competencies

## Dimensions (4)
1. Software Engineering & Design
2. Execution & Results
3. Collaboration & Communication
4. Community & Citizenship

## Levels (5 IC)
- Software Engineering Apprentice
- Software Engineer
- Software Engineer 2
- Senior Software Engineer
- Staff Software Engineer

(Separate `managers.md` exists)

## Format
- Behavioral expectations per cell
- Higher levels gated by "established a track record" — 3-4 quarters of consistent demonstration

## Principle
- Engineers don't need to hit all competencies simultaneously
- "Over time one demonstrates proficiency in all areas"
- Manager + engineer collaboratively pick growth areas
