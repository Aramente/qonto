# Notion — Career framework templates

Source: https://www.notion.com/templates/skills-competency-matrix and category pages

## Pattern
Notion templates model career frameworks as **linked databases**:
- Skills/Competencies DB
- Proficiency Levels DB
- Roles DB
- Self-assessments DB
- Learning paths / IDPs DB

## Features common to the better templates
- Customizable categories
- Proficiency levels (typically 3-5)
- Self-assessment workflow
- Filtering / boards / timelines
- "Track and record new skills as you develop them"

## Specific example: Product Marketing Career Framework
- 4 IC roles: Associate PMM, PMM, Senior PMM, Principal PMM
- General competencies + skill definitions + responsibilities per role
- Notion-native: each role is a page, each competency is a page, relations between them

## Insight for Qonto
Notion validates the **graph model in user-facing tooling** — non-technical teams already model frameworks as linked databases (not flat sheets). The shape Qonto needs is already a familiar mental model.
