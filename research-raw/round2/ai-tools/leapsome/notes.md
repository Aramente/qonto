# Leapsome AI (added as substitute for dead HuddleUp domain)

URL: https://www.leapsome.com/product/leapsome-ai

## Features (WebSearch)
- **AI Review Assistant** — voice + typed input, guided prompts, reframing suggestions, evidence-based drafts
- **AI Copilot** — pulls past feedback, goals, achievements during review writing
- **Meeting Coach** — transcript analysis to coach meeting quality
- **AI-generated analytics widgets** — natural-language queries → chart
- **AI Widget Builder** — interpret analytics with natural language
- All AI text **fully editable**, framed as suggestions, not drafts

## Trust pattern
Explicit positioning: "Rather than automatically drafting full reviews based on historical data, Leapsome AI acts as a smart assistant by providing prompts, suggestions, and summaries while keeping users in full control. All AI-generated text is editable." Strongest "augment not decide" framing in the set.

## Anti-patterns
None obvious — they actively avoid the full-draft approach Macorva embraces.
