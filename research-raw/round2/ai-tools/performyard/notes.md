# PerformYard

URL: https://www.performyard.com/

## Features (from defuddle)
- **AI-assisted 1:1 summaries** — actionable summary per 1:1
- **AI-assisted feedback delivery** — "deliver better, unbiased feedback with AI-assist"
- **AI-backed narratives for C-suite** — narrative summarization of org-wide perf data
- **Flexible review cycles** — quarterly, semi-annual, project-based, 360
- **Compensation clarity for employees** — "gain clarity on comp rationale" (process transparency, not AI pricing)
- Explicit positioning: **"human interaction over algorithms"** — anti-algorithmic-decisioning stance

## Trust pattern
The marketing leans on "human interaction over algorithms" — they explicitly differentiate from full-stack AI competitors. AI is positioned as assistive only.

## Anti-patterns
"AI-backed narrative for the board" — if this aggregates individuals into a story the board acts on, the individuals never see it. Org-level fine, individual-level risky.
