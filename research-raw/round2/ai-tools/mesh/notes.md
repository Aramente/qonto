# Mesh AI — Maven (manager coaching agent)

URL: https://www.mesh.ai/platform/maven-ai-coaching-copilot

## Features (from WebSearch)
- **Real-time coaching at moment of need** — personalized prompts for managers
- **Goal-setting guidance** — high-quality goal writing aligned to business objectives, SMART
- **1:1 meeting support** — transcribes calls, auto-populates agenda points, recommends topics for next 1:1
- **Feedback quality enhancement** — assists in writing meaningful praise/advice; "insightful, evidence-based reviews that are free from bias"
- **Post-review action plans** — tailored coaching after each review, transforming feedback into growth plans
- **COIN framework** for actionable goal-setting and feedback recommendations
- **Employee coaching** — teaches employees to give feedback, set SMART goals

## Trust pattern
Coaching is framed as "recommendations" using a named, citable framework (COIN). Evidence-based reviews — implies the AI surfaces source feedback.

## Anti-patterns
Borderline: "Maven... helps deliver insightful, evidence-based reviews that are free from bias and tailored to resonate with your team." If it drafts the review verdict vs. assembling evidence, it crosses the line. Their post-review action-plan "data-backed recommendations" needs human gate.
