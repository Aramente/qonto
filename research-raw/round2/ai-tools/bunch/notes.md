# Bunch.ai

URL: https://bunch.ai/

## Features (from defuddle)
- **Daily leadership-tip nudge** — personalized to your leadership-style profile + current goals
- **Bunchee AI coach** — conversational, situational ("how can I improve team communication?")
- **Leadership-style discovery** — 12+ archetypes (Maker, Guardian, Specialist, etc.)
- **Peer-resonance signal** — "learn from peers by seeing what resonates with them"
- **Mobile-first** (iOS), 1-minute daily commitment

## Trust pattern
Coaching is general-purpose tips, not employee-specific verdicts. No mention of integrating with org HRIS — so it can't pretend to know someone it doesn't.

## Anti-patterns
Archetype labeling — assigning "you are The Maverick" is a soft form of typing. Fine for self-reflection, dangerous if shared up to HR/comp.
