# Effy AI

URL: https://www.effy.ai/features

## Features (WebSearch)
- **Auto-generated review questions** — based on role + review type, in 90 seconds
- **Follow-up prompts for open-ended responses** — pushes reviewers to add specificity
- **Instant written summaries of 360 review cycles** — highlights themes, strengths, dev areas
- **Self-evaluation flow** — employees submit self-assessments
- **Status tracking** for review completion

## Trust pattern
AI summary as a *draft of themes* across many feedback inputs, not a verdict. Reviewer still writes the actual content.

## Anti-patterns
The follow-up prompts engine, if mandatory, could pressure reviewers into manufacturing detail they don't have. Borderline.
