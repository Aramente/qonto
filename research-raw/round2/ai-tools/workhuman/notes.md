# Workhuman AI Assistant (Workhuman iQ + Human Intelligence)

URL: https://www.workhuman.com/products/ai-assistant/

## Features (WebSearch)
- **Recognition-writing help** — refine wording when writing recognition for a colleague
- **Bias detection in recognition** — flags biased language, suggests inclusive alternatives in real time (DEI Coach)
- **Post-recognition stats** — shows retention/mindset deltas after the recognition is sent (post-hoc, not predictive)
- **Talent & skills intelligence from recognition data** — surfaces emerging skills/strengths from recognition trends, without self-reporting
- **People & culture Q&A assistant** — natural-language queries on recognition-grounded data

## Trust pattern
- Recognition-specific language model (their own, not GPT-by-default) trained on their corpus
- Real-time bias coach surfaces alternatives but user writes
- Skills inferred from real activity (recognition messages), not self-claim

## Anti-patterns
**"Talent intelligence surfacing emerging skills/strengths"** — if this feeds a hidden talent grade, dangerous. If it's only "people often praise Maria for X, here are her strengths in her own profile she can edit," that's augment.
