# Spinach AI (for managers)

URL: https://www.spinach.ai/ (use-cases/managers 404, see WebSearch)

## Features
- **Meeting summary** structured as Action items / Blockers / Celebrations
- **Action items auto-extracted** from transcript with speaker attribution → routed to PM tool
- **Slack-native delivery** — summary lands in Slack channel, no separate app
- **1:1 summaries** — works on 15-min 1:1s
- **100+ languages** + SOC2/GDPR/HIPAA

## Trust pattern
- Summary based strictly on transcript — quote-attached
- Action items attributed to the named speaker (not invented owners)
- No "recordings or transcripts buried in another app" — delivered where you work

## Anti-patterns
**Auto-routing to PM tools** is action-taking. If the AI mis-hears a commitment, it creates a phantom task assigned to a real person. Needs a confirmation step before push.
