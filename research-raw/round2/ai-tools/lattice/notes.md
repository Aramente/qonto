# Lattice AI / Coach

URL: https://lattice.com/ai

## Features (from product page, WebFetch)
- **AI Agent** — instant answers to company policy, career growth, people strategy
- **Manager Effectiveness** — flag burnout risks, guide career discussions
- **Performance Insights** — synthesize cross-functional feedback, goals, growth areas to help compose reviews
- **Engagement Insights** — analyze survey responses and open-ended comments → trends + recommended actions
- **Writing Assistance** — real-time grammar/clarity/bias guidance on reviews and feedback
- **Recommended Growth Plans** — generate IDPs based on career track, reviews, feedback
- **Team Health** — engagement trends + recommendations to reduce attrition

## Trust pattern
"AI enhancements are designed to empower people leaders (not machines!) to make informed decisions." Explicit: does NOT auto-generate reviews — assists in writing them. GDPR + SOC2 compliant. OpenAI model.

## Anti-patterns
None obvious from public page. The trend-aggregation engagement layer borders on "AI tells HR what's wrong" — okay if it's pattern-surfacing, risky if it auto-categorizes individuals.
