# Academic critique of forced distribution / stack ranking

Sources:
- Loberg, Nüesch, Foege (2021), "Forced distribution rating systems and team collaboration", J. Economic Behavior & Organization — https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0167268121001827
- Schleicher et al. (2016), "Precarious curve ahead: The effects of forced distribution rating systems on job performance"
- Springer Management Review Quarterly (2023) — systematic literature review.
- Planche (Univ. Guelph thesis) — "Rater Reactions to Forced Distribution Rating Systems"

## Headline findings
- Forced distribution → **lower citizenship behavior**, **higher counterproductive behavior** via injustice perceptions and dysfunctional competition.
- Short-term performance gains erode over time; risk increasingly outweighs benefit, especially under high task interdependence.
- Raters report greater difficulty and lower perceived fairness — especially when ratee performance variance is genuinely low ("nobody on my team belongs in the bottom 15%").
- **Bell-curve misapplication**: small teams (< 20) violate the statistical assumption of normality. Forcing the shape manufactures fake variance.
- **Team-collaboration effect**: FDRS **speeds up individual task completion** but **slows team task completion**. Aligns with stack-ranking-kills-collaboration anecdotes (Microsoft, GE).

## Anti-patterns for Qonto to avoid in the demo
- Hard quotas ("you must distribute 70/20/10").
- Mandatory bottom-rating with no escape valve.
- Distribution applied at team level (< 20) instead of org level.
- Using distribution shape as the gate to lock the cycle.

## What to do instead (per research)
- **Reference distribution as guidance**, not gate. Show org-level shape, allow team deviation with justification.
- Surface the **outliers in shape** ("this team rated 5 of 6 as top-tier") but let the manager defend the deviation; don't force a redistribution.
- Use rater-level patterns (Greenhouse-style) to spot consistently-lenient/strict managers across cycles, not just within one cycle.
