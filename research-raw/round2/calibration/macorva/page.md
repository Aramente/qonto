# Macorva — AI-driven calibration / review

Sources:
- https://www.macorva.com/blog/how-ai-reduces-bias-in-employee-performance-reviews
- https://www.macorva.com/features/ai-performance-review-workflow

## AI features
- **Radiant AI®** aggregates feedback, surveys, manager notes → instant performance materials.
- **Radiant AI® Risk Analysis** flags potentially biased language or unsupported statements **for manager review** (NOT auto-rewriting).
- **Source-cited AI drafts**: AI references the source data inside the review, so a manager can trace any claim.
- **Real-time bias monitoring** alerts itself and stakeholders to potential biases as they occur.

## Transparency mechanisms (Qonto-relevant — "help, don't decide")
- Contextual explanations: e.g. when rating cooperation, the AI cites "Your team involvement with three projects solicited significant feedback."
- Algorithmic transparency: company actively communicates how AI decisions are reached.
- Human oversight: managers **review** flagged concerns, do not accept AI determinations automatically.

## Quoted ROI
- ~2 hours saved per manager per cycle.
- 30x ROI in mid-market.
