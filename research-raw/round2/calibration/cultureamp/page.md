# Culture Amp — Calibration UX

Sources:
- https://support.cultureamp.com/en/articles/10425688-create-and-share-calibration-views
- https://support.cultureamp.com/en/articles/10368650-performance-management-product-updates-2025

## Primitives
- **Calibration View** — main canvas for HR/managers to review and adjust performance data across an employee group before finalization.
- **Ratings Matrix** — recently added to calibration views; visualizes distribution of performance ratings against another scale (9-box or 4x4). Targeted at HRBPs and Perf Admins.

## Multi-cycle comparison
- Compare up to **3 past performance cycles** directly within the calibration table — historical ratings inline, no profile toggling.

## Audit trail
- **Calibration Activity Log** tracks all changes made to performance and rating scale questions for transparency.

## Pre-meeting prep
- **Ratings Preview** — calibrators review and provide input on manager-assigned ratings **before** the live session. Differences identified early; live conversation focuses on disagreements.

## Bulk
- **Import calibration ratings** from off-platform (e.g., spreadsheet) bulk into a calibration view for review and publish.

## Bias-surfacing standout
- Three-cycle history side-by-side directly addresses recency bias — "is this rating consistent with the last two cycles?"
