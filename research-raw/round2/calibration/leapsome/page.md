## Three view modes
1. **Distribution Chart** — absolute counts of aggregate ratings (not %). Used when targeting a specific distribution.
2. **Heatmap View** — common for calibration meetings: surfaces teams/employees evaluated exceptionally high or low vs the rest. Direct comparison across teams/managers for fairness.
3. **Box Grid View** — pick the question for the x-axis and y-axis (any two review questions). Choose 'None' on one axis to calibrate a single dimension.

## Access
- Actions → Calibrate → choose review cycle.
- Admin or Manager calibration permissions.

## Bias-surfacing standout
- **Heatmap by team/manager** = the strongest "show me which team is rating too leniently" affordance in this set without naming individual bias.
