## Positioning
- French SIRH suite. Entretiens module covers annual + professional reviews compliant with French legal requirements (entretien professionnel obligatoire).
- **No dedicated calibration UI surfaced** in public docs — "harmonization" happens through:
  - Configurable interview forms (Likert, stars, competency grids, training requests).
  - **Group preparation** so managers prep at the same time → consistency.
  - Post-campaign analysis surfaces high potentials and informs comp / training plan.

## Gap vs Lattice/Confirm
- No 9-box, no real-time matrix calibration session UI documented.
- Calibration in Lucca is more "harmonized form design + post-hoc analysis" than a live calibration ritual.
- **Opportunity**: this is the French incumbent. A French-domiciled tool (Qonto) shipping a real calibration ritual would be net-new vs Lucca for French mid-market.
