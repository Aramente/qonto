## Core primitives
- **9-Box Grid** as primary calibration visualization (Performance × Potential axes; employee circles plotted by their assessment).
- **Box View** and **Calibration Table View** are two switchable modes on the same calibration group.
- **Calibration Groups**: segment reviewees into manageable cohorts; participants and facilitators are scoped to their group.

## Roles & permissions
- **Admins**: full visibility, can lock.
- **Facilitators**: scoped to group; can see initial + calibrated ratings, tags, notes, past reviews; can calibrate scores, download CSV, upload CSV updates.
- **Participants**: scoped read access depending on group settings.

## Calibration moves
- Move people inside the 9-Box (drag) or change rating in-table.
- CSV bulk-update + direct score adjustment supported.

## Comments
- Limited comment capability vs Confirm/Workday (per Confirm's comparison page); facilitator notes attached to group.

## April 2026 updates
- **Bulk configuration**: apply table settings + participant access across selected groups in a few clicks.
- **Direct sharing links** for calibration groups.
- **Embedded analytics**: distribution charts, average scores, heatmaps, box views, calibration analytics in one interface.
- **Auto-scoping** for managers and managers-of-managers to their own teams.
- **Cross-cycle rating trend tracking** in chart format (no admin reports needed).
- **Pending Requests** view in Feedback Log.

## Bias surfacing
- Basic statistical outlier detection (per Confirm's shootout — not advanced).
- "Reducing bias" pitched via "integrates data from multiple sources" / "balanced view."

## AI
- No AI-powered calibration features listed in April 2026 update.

## Multi-user real-time
- Not real-time multi-cursor; calibration happens in-app with CSV import/export options.

## Lock / sign-off
- Admin locks the session; locked state freezes calibrated ratings into the published review.
