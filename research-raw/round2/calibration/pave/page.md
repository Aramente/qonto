## Comp-side calibration UI
- **In-app commenting** on approval flows + historical access logs to keep comp reviews aligned.
- **Manager-friendly UI** + built-in guidance so managers understand decisions and can stand behind recommendations.
- Customizable guideline ranges: salary, bonus, equity refreshers.
- Centralized portal to **analyze compensation fairness** + run org-wide comp reviews.

## Why it matters for Qonto perf
- Comp calibration is the **next step downstream** of performance calibration. Same patterns:
  - Distribution-aware budget envelope.
  - In-context commenting on individual decisions.
  - Approval flow with audit trail.
- The "manager confidence" framing — "stand behind every recommendation" — is the **right tone** for Qonto's "help, don't decide" positioning.
