# Workday — Calibration UX

## Two distinct workflows
- **Performance Calibration** — ratings distribution for comp.
- **Talent Calibration** — high-potential / succession identification.

## Roles
- **Facilitation** role (typically HR) runs calibration sessions on behalf of leaders.

## Calibration Comments (2025 / R2 update)
- Dedicated **"Comments" column** appears once Activity Stream for Calibration is enabled.
- Click into the comment box → see history → add comment → **tag employees** involved.
- Complete history with **user attribution** per entry.
- Before this: notes piggybacked on "Assess Potential" → risk of overwriting potential notes. Now separated.

## Bias surfacing
- Statistical rating outlier detection (AI-powered, per Confirm's shootout).
- Manual configuration required for prioritization.

## Multi-level / cross-org
- Strong support for multi-level / cross-divisional sessions, custom approval chains, integration with comp planning.
- Most configurable for 10k+ enterprises.

## Bulk
- Full bulk adjustment capability.

## Distribution viz
- Bell curve + 9-box views.

## Deployment
- 3–9 months typical implementation.

## Anti-pattern (from sources)
- "Process-heavy by design" — calibration comments improved in-session collaboration but underlying workflow is still rigid.
