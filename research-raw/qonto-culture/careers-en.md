## Where Qonto came from

Qonto was born out of frustration. As new entrepreneurs, Alexandre and Steve felt bogged down by their own, slow, complex banking experience, which lacked cutting edge tools for progressive companies. So, they decided to create a banking product fit for the modern entrepreneur. One that, when you use it, just seems to "click".

We’re dusting off the old banking experience and giving small businesses the freedom to succeed. We're driven by a desire to save our customers time. Time that they can reclaim for forging their own future. And that’s something we think is pretty valuable.

## Surround yourself with talented colleagues

Through their talent and motivation, our teams have earned the trust of more than 600,000 customers. Every Qontoer contributes daily to our mission: to make life easier for entrepreneurs. Join us!
