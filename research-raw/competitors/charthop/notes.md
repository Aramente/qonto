# ChartHop — raw research notes

Source: https://www.charthop.com/

## Features
- Real-time visual org planning — "command center for people data".
- 7 modules: HRIS, Headcount Planning, Compensation, Performance, Engagement, Goals, AI Pro.
- Comp: "fair, transparent, budget-aware pay cycles".
- Perf: "integrated cycles that people actually (want to) complete" — emphase engagement.
- Conversational AI pour insights.

## Signature UX
- **Org chart-first** UX — toute action s'ancre sur la position dans l'org chart.
- Visuel-lourd, dashboard-driven.
