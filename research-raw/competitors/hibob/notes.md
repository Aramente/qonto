# HiBob — raw research notes

Source: https://www.hibob.com/performance-management/

## Features
- Performance lives under "Talent" section.
- SMART goals framework.
- 360° feedback.
- Recurring check-ins (weekly/biweekly) — coaching cadence vs annual.
- 1:1 templates.
- Recognition + rewards integrated.
- Compensation planning + events.

## Signature UX
- HRIS-first, perf is a module — feels embedded in the broader employee record.
- Talent section is the unifier.
- Continuous-coaching positioning, soft-pedals annual reviews.
