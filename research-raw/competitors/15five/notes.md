# 15Five — raw research notes

Source: https://www.15five.com/

## Features
- Weekly check-ins (the original 15Five feature: 15 min to write, 5 to read).
- Performance reviews AI-assisted, bias-reduction language.
- OKRs + goal tracking.
- Comp insights linked to perf.
- 9-box Talent Matrix in Growth Studio.
- Kona AI Assistant: manager coaching.
- AMAYA AI agent: Q&A on people data.
- Manager microlearnings.

## Signature UX
- Weekly cadence baked into UX — every screen pulls from continuous data, not annual snapshots.
- Outcomes Dashboard centerpiece.
