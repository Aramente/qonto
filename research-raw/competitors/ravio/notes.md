# Ravio — raw research notes

Sources:
- https://ravio.com/
- https://ravio.com/compensation-reviews

## Features
- Real-time market data: 1,500+ companies, 48+ geographies, 300+ roles.
- Salary bands avec compa-ratio.
- **Merit matrix** intégrée (rating × position-in-range → recommandation).
- Pay equity / pay-gap detection (EU Pay Transparency Directive).
- Comp reviews: live employee data + market + bands + matrice + budgets + approval, le tout en un seul endroit.
- Adapte la logique recommandation, budget, approval flow, eligibility au framework de l'entreprise.
- HRIS sync auto (joiners, promo, exits, role changes).

## Signature UX
- "Built exactly as a reward professional would want it" — positionnement vs Pave (US-flavored).
- "Real-time data with amazing visualisations" — accent UX explicite.
- Plus européen / UK-FR friendly que Pave.
