# Workleap / Officevibe — raw research notes

## Features
- Pulse surveys + custom feedback collection.
- "Good Vibes" recognition cards (peer-to-peer).
- 1:1 agendas.

- AI recommended actions from survey results.

- Slack/Teams integration heavy.
