# Culture Amp — raw research notes

Source: https://www.cultureamp.com/platform/performance-management

## Features
- 3 pillars: Engage / Perform / Develop.
- Calibration + centralized point-in-time feedback to mitigate bias.
- 360° real-time feedback.
- 1:1s with templates.
- AI Coach: summarizes perf data, drafts objective review language, offers role-play prep for tough conversations.
- "Performance Culture Quadrant" — AI-coached leadership behaviors framework.

## Signature UX
- Engagement-first heritage shows: surveys are deeply integrated into perf.
- Coach metaphor (role-play prep) is unique.
