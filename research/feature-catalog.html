Help everyone do their job.
Don't make decisions for them.

A breadth-first feature catalog for Qonto's Workday replacement. Built under one rule: AI never produces a judgment-bearing output. It retrieves, synthesizes, drafts, and translates. The Qontoer, the manager, and the People team stay in the driver's seat — always.

Employee

Make your case.

Find your evidence. Draft your story. Show your trajectory. Own every word that ends up in your file.

Manager

Do your job, with help.

See the team in one place. Get every relevant piece of evidence one click away. Write the review yourself, faster, calmer.

Peer

Contribute, fast.

Respond in minutes. Anonymity per question. See your own praise history. Feedback should never feel like a tax.

People team

See patterns, not verdicts.

Spot the cycle-wide signal — bias, equity, completion. The system surfaces; the People team decides what to do.

What's in scope · what's out

Locked by Kevin · 2026-05-12. Every feature in this catalog respects these constraints. Features that violate them are listed in the Rejected per philosophy appendix at the bottom.

In scope

  • Performance review (bi-annual Progress Review)
  • Compensation review (salary grid + skill matrix)
  • Calibration (cross-team fairness check)
  • Pure-augment AI — drafts, summarises, retrieves, translates
  • Standard HR data — HRIS, calendar, goals, peer praise, manager notes
  • 8 countries — FR / DE / IT / ES / AT / BE / NL / PT

Out of scope

  • Hiring, onboarding, exit, succession, L&D, mobility marketplace
  • AI that proposes ratings, comp numbers, or rankings
  • Surveillance-flavoured data sources (Slack, email, code)
